# Task 3.1 – States

> Question: Identify all states and give each an ID, meaning, entry condition
> and exit condition.

| ID | State | Meaning | Entry Condition | Exit Condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | IDLE | Powered on at the warehouse, waiting for a request | Power on, or WarehouseReached | DeliveryRequest, SafeModeTrigger |
| S2 | NAVIGATING | Moving towards the destination clinic | DeliveryRequest (from IDLE), ObstacleCleared, LinkRestored | ObstacleDetected, DestinationReached, CriticalBattery, LinkLost, SafeModeTrigger |
| S3 | AVOIDING_OBSTACLE | Navigation suspended while handling an obstacle | ObstacleDetected | ObstacleCleared, CriticalBattery, LinkLost, SafeModeTrigger |
| S4 | DELIVERING | Stopped at the clinic, handing over the batch | DestinationReached (from NAVIGATING only) | DeliveryComplete, SafeModeTrigger |
| S5 | RETURNING | Travelling back to the warehouse | DeliveryComplete, CriticalBattery, SafeModeCleared, LinkRestored | WarehouseReached, ObstacleDetected, LinkLost, SafeModeTrigger |
| S6 | SAFE_MODE | Stopped at a safe spot, chamber kept powered, only Supervisor commands accepted | SafeModeTrigger (within 3 s – CR-01) | SafeModeCleared |
| S7 | LINK_LOST | No message from MCC for 60 s; robot holds at a safe spot | LinkLost | LinkRestored, SafeModeTrigger, CriticalBattery |

S1–S5 come from the scenario. S6 and S7 are the extension (see 3.4).

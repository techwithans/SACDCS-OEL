# Task 2.2 – Why the New Wording Is More Measurable / Testable

> Question: Explain, for each CR, why the new wording is more measurable or more
> testable than the original.

## CR-01 – Safe Mode (FR-08)
- **Old problem:** No time limit. "Critical condition" had no clear value, so a
  tester could not decide pass or fail.
- **Now:** It has a fixed time (3 s) and fixed thresholds (0 °C, 12 °C, 60 °C, 10 %).
- **How to test:** Inject a chamber reading of 13 °C and measure the time until
  Safe Mode. 3 s or less = PASS, more = FAIL.
- **Extra:** Battery temperature was missing before; now it is covered.

## CR-02 – Number of Robots (NFR-03)
- **Old problem:** "Multiple" could mean 2 or 200, so no test could fail it.
- **Now:** "At least 20" is a clear number.
- **How to test:** Load test with 20 connected robots and check that NFR-01
  timings still hold. Clear PASS / FAIL.

## CR-03 – Authorisation (FR-02)
- **Old problem:** Only one check (logged in or not). A logged-in Viewer could
  still send a Dispatch command and the test would pass.
- **Now:** Two checks: authenticated **and** role allowed.
- **How to test:** A Viewer who is logged in sends Dispatch. The command must
  be rejected and logged. One test case per role gives full coverage.

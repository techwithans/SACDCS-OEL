# Task 1.2 – Non-Functional Requirements

> Question: Identify 5 Non-Functional Requirements (performance, security,
> scalability, reliability, usability). NFRs must be measurable.

| Req. ID | Requirement | Type | Priority | Verification Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NFR-01 (Performance) | The robot shall acknowledge a valid command within 10 seconds, and the Mission Control dashboard shall show new telemetry within 15 seconds of it being sent. | NFR | High | Test (timing measurement) |
| NFR-02 (Security) | All commands and telemetry shall be encrypted with TLS 1.3. After 3 wrong login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes, and idle sessions end after 15 minutes. | NFR | Critical | Test + Inspection |
| NFR-03 (Scalability) | The system shall support communication with multiple robots simultaneously. | NFR | Medium | Test (load testing) |
| NFR-04 (Reliability) | The onboard system shall be available for at least 99.5 % of delivery hours per month, and no log data shall be lost during a link outage of up to 60 minutes. | NFR | High | Analysis + Test |
| NFR-05 (Usability) | A new operator shall reach any robot's status on the web portal in 3 clicks or fewer, and each page shall load within 3 seconds on a 2 Mbps connection. | NFR | Medium | User testing |

> **Note:** NFR-03 is kept in its approved baseline wording. It is made measurable
> by CR-02 in Task 2 (version v1.1).

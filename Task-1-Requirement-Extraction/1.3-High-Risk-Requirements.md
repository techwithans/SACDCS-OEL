# Task 1.3 – High-Risk Requirements

> Question: Identify 4 High-Risk Requirements (safety-critical, real-time,
> link-dependent, or railway-crossing related).

| Req. ID | Requirement | Type | Priority | Verification Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HR-01 (Safety-critical – thermal) | If chamber temperature is still outside 2–8 °C after the 10-minute recovery period, the chamber shall enter Protection Mode, mark the batch as "Quarantined" so it cannot be handed over, and alert the operator. | Safety | Critical | Test (fault injection) + FMEA |
| HR-02 (Link-dependent) | The robot shall reject any movement command older than 15 seconds. If no valid message arrives from Mission Control for 60 seconds, it shall stop at the nearest safe spot (LINK_LOST). | Safety | Critical | Test (link delay/loss simulator) |
| HR-03 (Railway-crossing) | If ARLCCS does not reply within 10 seconds, or its signal is lost, the robot shall treat the crossing as unsafe and wait. A robot already on the tracks shall first clear the tracks, and only then stop or enter Safe Mode. | Safety | Critical | Test (ARLCCS simulator) + Analysis |
| HR-04 (Real-time) | When a person or object is detected within 1 metre, the robot shall stop within 500 milliseconds using onboard logic, without waiting for Mission Control. | Safety | Critical | Test (timing on hardware) |

## Why these are high-risk
- **HR-01:** A spoiled vaccine given to a patient can cause harm.
- **HR-02:** Link delay means old commands may arrive when they are no longer safe.
- **HR-03:** A robot stopped on railway tracks can cause a train accident.
- **HR-04:** Waiting several seconds for Mission Control could injure a person.

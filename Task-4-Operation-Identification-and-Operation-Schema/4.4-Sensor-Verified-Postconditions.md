# Task 4.4 – Postconditions That Must Be Verified by Sensor Readings

> Question: Identify two operations whose postcondition must be verified by sensor
> readings rather than assumed after a command is issued, and explain why.

## 1. OP-05 CorrectTemperature
- **Command issued:** Cooler ON.
- **Wrong assumption:** "Cooler is on, so the temperature is now fine."
- **Why that is unsafe:**
  - The cooler may be broken.
  - The door seal may be leaking.
  - The outside heat may be too high for the cooler.
- **Correct postcondition:** The temperature **sensor reads 2–8 °C** within 10 minutes (checked by OP-07).
- **If not verified:** Warm vaccines would be delivered as "safe".

## 2. OP-12 VerifyAndResumeConservation
- **Command issued:** Door closed, resume conservation.
- **Wrong assumption:** "Door is closed, so everything is back to normal."
- **Why that is unsafe:**
  - The temperature may have risen while the door was open.
  - A sensor may have been damaged or moved.
  - The scenario says operation must not resume until conditions **and** sensors are verified.
- **Correct postcondition:** Door sensor = closed ∧ temperature and humidity in range ∧ all sensors pass a health check.
- **If not verified:** The chamber could run "normally" with wrong readings and nobody would notice the medicine spoiling.

## Key idea
**A command only shows what we asked for. A sensor shows what actually happened.**
In a safety-critical system, success must be proven by measurement, not assumed.

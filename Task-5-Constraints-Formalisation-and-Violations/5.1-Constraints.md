# Task 5.1 – Constraints

> Question: Identify at least 10 constraints the combined system must always
> satisfy. For each give: Constraint ID, constraint in simple English, and why
> it is necessary. Include at least two constraints linking the robot and the
> chamber, and one for sensor failure or communication loss.

| C-ID | Constraint (simple English) | Why it is necessary | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | The barrier must not be open when a train is approaching or on the crossing. | Prevents collision with vehicles and robots | Railway |
| C-02 | Warning lights and alarm must be on when a train is approaching. | Warns everyone near the crossing | Railway |
| C-03 | The barrier may open only after the crossing is confirmed clear. | A train may still be passing | Railway |
| C-04 | The robot may enter the crossing only if the barrier is open, no train is approaching, and ARLCCS has given permission. | Main safety rule for the robot | Robot + Railway |
| C-05 | The robot must not stay on the crossing for more than 20 seconds. | The robot must never stop on the tracks | Robot + Railway |
| C-06 | If the train sensor fails, the barrier stays closed and the operator is alerted. | Fail-safe: unknown means unsafe | **Sensor failure** |
| C-07 | If the robot–ARLCCS link is lost, the robot must not enter the crossing. | No confirmation means unsafe | **Communication loss** |
| C-08 | If the barrier fails (actual position differs from command after 10 s), no crossing permission is given. | Barrier cannot be trusted | Railway fault |
| C-09 | If two train sensors disagree, the system must assume a train is coming. | Handles incorrect readings | Incorrect readings |
| C-10 | The chamber door must be closed while the robot is moving. | Protects temperature and prevents theft | **Robot + Chamber** |
| C-11 | A batch may be handed over only when the chamber is safe and the robot is delivering. | No spoiled vaccine reaches patients | **Robot + Chamber** |
| C-12 | The robot may be in DELIVERING only when it is stopped at the destination. | Never deliver while moving or avoiding | Robot |

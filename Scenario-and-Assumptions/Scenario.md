## Key parts of the system
- Delivery robot life-cycle (idle, navigating, obstacle avoidance, delivering, returning)
- Conservation chamber (temperature, humidity, vibration, door, power)
- Low-bandwidth remote link with several seconds delay
- Operator authentication and role-based authorisation
- Safe Mode for critical battery, thermal or fault conditions
- Railway level-crossings controlled by ARLCCS
- Static web portal
